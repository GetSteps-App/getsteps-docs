AI Coach reads your workout and gives you personalized feedback — a short assessment, numbered tips to improve, and a suggestion for next time. The assessment opens inside [Workout Chat](/features/workout-chat), not as a separate block on the detail screen.

<Note>AI Coach uses Apple Intelligence on an iPhone that supports it, on iOS 26 or later. On iOS 27, feedback can use Private Cloud Compute. The assessment is free for everyone; **Tips** and **Try next time** require Steps Pro.</Note>

Open any workout from your history and tap the Apple Intelligence button on the workout header. Once your workout metrics finish loading, AI Coach generates feedback from that activity — including heart rate, pace, splits, distance, elevation, and duration — and from your recent history of the same kind of workout.

The feedback is tailored to the type of workout, so the advice you get for a run differs from a ride or a strength session. Very short sessions still open the chat, but a full assessment waits until the workout is long enough to say something useful.

## What You Get

- **Assessment** — A short summary of how your workout went. Free for everyone.
- **Tips** — Numbered, actionable suggestions to help you improve. *(Steps Pro)*
- **Try next time** — One idea to focus on in your next session. *(Steps Pro)*

While the feedback is being prepared, you'll see an **Analyzing your workout…** message. You can refresh to regenerate the feedback at any time.

## Chat About Your Workout

The same conversation holds the assessment and your follow-up questions. See [Workout Chat](/features/workout-chat) for reminders, tables, and how Pro gates the composer.

<Tip>The more complete your workout data (heart rate, route, splits, effort), the more specific the feedback.</Tip>
