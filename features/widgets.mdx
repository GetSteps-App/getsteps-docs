Steps offers a variety of widgets so you can see your step count, streaks, and workout data without opening the app.

## Widget Types

| Widget | Sizes | Plan | Description |
|--------|-------|------|-------------|
| **Today Steps** | Small, Lock Screen (rectangular, inline, circular) | Free | Current step count and goal progress |
| **Streak** | Small, Medium | Free | Current streak count and weekly goal progress |
| **Stepboard Ranking** | Small | Free | Today's top 5 on the leaderboard plus your rank |

| **Quick Start Workouts** | Medium | Free | Your main workout plus shortcuts for three more types (iOS 26+) |
| **Hourly Chart** | Medium | Pro | Bar chart of steps by hour for today |
| **Weekly Chart** | Small, Medium | Pro | Bar chart of steps for the past 7 days |
| **Monthly Chart** | Medium | Pro | Bar chart of steps for the current month |
| **Monthly Calendar** | Small | Pro | Calendar grid showing daily goal completion for the month |
| **Yearly Calendar** | Medium, Large | Pro | Year-at-a-glance calendar with goal completion heatmap |

| **Workout Calendar** | Medium, Large, Extra Large | Pro | Trained days marked with that day's activity |

<Note>Pro widgets can still be added without a subscription — they show a prompt to unlock Steps Pro until you upgrade.</Note>

## Stepboard Ranking Widget

<Frame caption="Stepboard Ranking widget showing today's top 5 and your rank">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/widgets-stepboard-ranking.jpg" alt="Stepboard Ranking widget" />
</Frame>

The Stepboard Ranking widget puts your [Stepboard](/features/stepboard) on your Home Screen. It shows today's top 5 walkers with medal emojis for the leaders, plus your own rank if you're outside the top 5. When you add the widget, you can choose which board it displays — the World board, your country's board, or any group you've joined. Tap the widget to jump straight into that board.

## Start Workout Widget

<Frame caption="Start Workout widget for one-tap workout tracking">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/widgets-start-workout.jpg" alt="Start Workout widget" />
</Frame>

The Start Workout widgets let you begin tracking a [workout](/features/workout-session) straight from your Home Screen. Tap one and Steps opens directly into that workout type — no menus to dig through. Both require iOS 26 or later.

- **Start Workout** (small) — a single configurable workout type with a Start button
- **Quick Start Workouts** (medium) — your main workout plus quick-start shortcuts for three more types

When you add the widget, choose which workout type it starts. The same types you can start in the app are available, including strength training, HIIT, yoga, and the rest of the [workout list](/features/workout-session#workout-types). If a workout is already in progress, tapping the widget opens the active session instead.

## Workout Calendar Widget

The Workout Calendar widget marks each day you trained with that day's activity, so a month of running, walking, and strength work reads at a glance.

- **Medium** and **Large** are available on every supported iPhone
- **Extra Large** is available on iOS 27

<Note>The Workout Calendar widget is a Pro feature.</Note>

## Yearly Goal Widget

The Yearly Goal widget tracks your progress toward your [yearly step goal](/features/yearly-goal). It shows a progress bar, whether you're on track or behind, and supporting stats. It's available in small and medium sizes.

## Live Activity

During an active workout, a Live Activity appears on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (iOS 26+). It shows:

- Workout type icon
- Elapsed time
- Distance covered
- Calories burned
- Current pace

The Dynamic Island cycles between stats every 30 seconds. On iOS 27, an active workout also shows in the landscape Dynamic Island and in StandBy.

## Adding Widgets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Long-press your Home Screen">
    Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen until the apps start jiggling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the + button">
    Tap the **+** button in the top-left corner to open the widget gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for Steps">
    Type "Steps" in the search bar to find all available widgets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a widget">
    Select the widget type and size you want, then tap **Add Widget**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lock Screen Widgets

<Frame caption="Steps Lock Screen widgets in three styles">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/widgets-lock-screen.jpg" alt="Lock Screen widgets" />
</Frame>

The Today Steps widget is also available for your Lock Screen in three styles:

- **Rectangular** - Shows step count and progress bar
- **Inline** - Compact step count in the date line
- **Circular** - Step count in a round gauge

<Tip>Your widgets automatically update with your latest step data and reflect your chosen color theme.</Tip>

## Keeping Widgets Up to Date

Step widgets read today's and yesterday's steps straight from Apple Health each time they refresh, so they stay current even if you haven't opened Steps in a while. Streaks stay accurate across midnight too.

<Note>Apple Health data can't be read while your iPhone is locked. During that time, widgets show the last steps Steps recorded and catch up the next time they refresh after you unlock.</Note>

<Tip>Keeping **Background App Refresh** on for Steps (**Settings → General → Background App Refresh**) lets the app sync your full history in the background, which keeps charts and calendar widgets up to date too.</Tip>
