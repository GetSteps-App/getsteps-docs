Steps makes it easy to start tracking your daily steps, workouts, and fitness goals right from your iPhone.

## Onboarding

When you first open Steps, a short setup gets you ready to go:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Steps How Active You Are">
    Pick **Light**, **Moderate**, **Active**, or **Intense**. Steps uses your answer to suggest a daily goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Apple Health">
    Tap **Connect Apple Health**, then **Allow All** so Steps can read your steps, distance, calories, flights climbed, and workouts from your iPhone and Apple Watch. If you turned access off, tap **Open Settings**, allow it under **Privacy → Health**, then tap **I've enabled it — check again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See Your Last 7 Days">
    Once Apple Health is connected, Steps shows the steps, calories, and workouts you've already logged this past week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Daily Goal">
    The picker starts at the goal suggested for your activity level, from 5,000 steps for Light up to 15,000 for Intense. Choose anything between 1,000 and 20,000 steps. You can always change it later in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Stepboard">
    Sign in with Apple and join the daily leaderboard, or skip it and join later from Home. If you already have an account, the button matches that: **Join Stepboard** when you're signed in, **Continue** when you're already a member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet App Lock">
    A quick introduction to [App Lock](/features/app-lock), which keeps distracting apps locked until you reach your step goal. Set it up whenever you're ready in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Notifications">
    Tap **Turn on notifications** for daily reminders, a celebration when you hit your goal, and a heads-up when your apps unlock. Tap **Maybe later** to skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Walking">
    That's it! Steps begins tracking automatically using your iPhone's motion sensors and Apple Health data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Between your goal and the Stepboard, a short **Featured by Apple** screen appears. The Stepboard step tells you how many walkers share your country when that number is available. Apple Health is the only step you can't skip: [Stepboard](/features/stepboard), [App Lock](/features/app-lock), and [Notifications](/features/notifications) can all be set up later.

Steps reads the following data from Apple Health:

- **Steps** - Your daily step count
- **Walking + Running Distance** - Distance covered on foot
- **Active Energy Burned** - Calories burned through activity
- **Flights Climbed** - Floors you've climbed
- **Heart Rate** - Used during workouts, including your heart rate zones
- **Workout Routes** - GPS routes for your recorded workouts
- **Workout Effort** - The 1–10 rating you set on a workout

<Tip>Rating effort writes that score back to Apple Health, and deleting a workout removes it from Apple Health. Everything else Steps shows is read from the health data already on your device.</Tip>

## Privacy

Your health data stays on your device. Steps does not upload your fitness data to any server. All processing happens locally on your iPhone.

## Re-syncing Health Data

If your step count looks incorrect or data appears missing, you can re-sync from Apple Health:

1. Open **Settings** in Steps
2. Scroll to the Support section
3. Tap **Re-sync Apple Health data**

This refreshes all your activity data from Apple Health.

## Restart Onboarding

To go through the setup flow again:

1. Open **Settings** in Steps
2. Tap **Restart Onboarding**
3. Confirm when prompted

This lets you reconfigure your daily goal and permissions.
