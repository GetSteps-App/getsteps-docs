Steps integrates with Siri and Spotlight so you can check your steps, open a workout, and share what is on screen without digging through the app.

## Available Shortcuts

### Today's Steps

Ask Siri for your current step count. Steps will respond with your total steps for today.

### Open Last Workout

Say **"Show my last workout in Steps"** and Siri opens that workout's detail view.

### Search and Spotlight

On iOS 27, your workouts and Stepboards are indexed for Spotlight. You can ask Siri to search your workouts — for example a long run last month — and Steps opens the [Activity](/features/activity-list) search with that phrase. Tapping a Spotlight result opens the matching workout or Stepboard.

Siri can also hand the share card you are looking at to Messages, Notes, or any app that accepts a photo. That works for both the workout card and the home step card.

Simply say any of these phrases to Siri:

- "Get my steps from Steps"
- "How many steps today in Steps"
- "What's my step count in Steps"
- "Show my steps from Steps"
- "Check my steps in Steps"
- "How far have I walked in Steps"

Siri recognizes many natural variations of these phrases, so you don't have to match the wording exactly.

<Tip>You can also add "Today's Steps" to the Shortcuts app to create custom automations or add it to your Home Screen.</Tip>

## Setting Up

The Steps shortcut is available automatically — no setup needed. Just make sure Siri is enabled on your device.

To find it in the Shortcuts app:

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app
2. Tap the **Apps** tab
3. Find **Steps**
4. Tap **Today's Steps** or **Open last workout** to add it to your shortcuts
